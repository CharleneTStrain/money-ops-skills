# Market downturn plan

**The problem:** When the market drops, the advice is "stay the course," which doesn't help if your house money is in stocks, your tips just dried up, or you retire next year. Most people make their biggest money decisions in the worst week to make them.

**What it does:** Sorts your money by when you need it: the next 2 years (kept safe), 2 to 5 years (matched to the date), and 5+ years (can ride it out). It checks the risks that actually hurt people in a downturn, like losing income, too much in one company, crypto or borrowing to invest, missing insurance, and scams. It tests what a drop would do to your accounts in dollars, then writes your own rules for what you'll do and won't do, while you're calm. It looks up this year's limits and rates instead of guessing, and never predicts the market.

**Use it with:** your accounts and what they hold, when you'll need the money, your income, your emergency fund and your insurance. "I don't know what I'm invested in" is a fine place to start.

**You get:** one thing to do this week, a 60-second version to reread mid-drop, and an Excel workbook with your three buckets, a drop test, a risk check, a "who to call" sheet and check-in alerts, plus a signed one-page "My investment rules." Education, not advice: it lays out the trade-offs and you decide.
