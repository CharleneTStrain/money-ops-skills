# Retirement and debt balance

**The problem:** Every dollar has five places it could go — the credit card, the 401(k), the emergency fund, the student loan, the kids' college fund — and most advice either picks one for you or assumes you have money left over.

**What it does:** Builds a priority ladder from your own numbers: minimum payments, a starter cushion, the full employer match, high-interest debt, a full emergency fund, retirement, then moderate debt, dated goals, and low-rate debt. Looks up this year's limits and credits instead of guessing them. If your pay doesn't cover the basics yet, it starts with benefits and credits you may qualify for instead. Built for households earning under $200K a year.

**Use it with:** your take-home pay and how often you're paid, essential expenses, each debt's balance and rate, and your employer's match. Rough numbers are fine.

**You get:** a one-line "your next dollar goes here," plus an Excel workbook with the full ladder, per-paycheck amounts, an avalanche vs. snowball payoff schedule, editable assumptions, a what-if view, sourced limits, and a monthly check-in tab. Education, not advice: it lays out the trade-offs and you decide.
