# Money ops context

**The problem:** Every money question starts with the same twenty questions — what you earn, how often you're paid, what you owe, what your employer matches. Answering them every time is tiring, and the answers drift.

**What it does:** Writes your household's money picture down once: who's in the household, income and pay schedule, essential expenses, debts, accounts, employer benefits, insurance, and goals. Every other skill reads it first. It never stores account numbers, Social Security numbers, or passwords.

**Use it with:** a pay stub, a budget spreadsheet, a benefits guide, or just a conversation. Ranges are fine.

**You get:** `.agents/money-ops-context.md`, a one-page file you can update whenever something changes.
