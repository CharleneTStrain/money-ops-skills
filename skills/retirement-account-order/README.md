# Retirement account order

**The problem:** 401(k), 403(b), 457(b), HSA, Roth IRA, traditional IRA. Every account has its own limit and tax rule, and most advice skips pensions, two-income households, and anyone who started late or is just starting.

**What it does:** Puts your accounts in order from your own numbers: the full employer match, an HSA if it makes sense for you, an IRA, then more workplace savings. It counts any pension contributions you already make, compares Roth and traditional fairly (including the effect on student loan payments), checks whether you're on track with savings, pension and Social Security together, and shows what starting now is worth. It looks up this year's limits instead of guessing.

**Use it with:** your age, pay and pay schedule, what your employer offers and matches, any pension or Social Security estimate, and what you've already saved. Rough numbers are fine.

**You get:** one thing to do this week, plus an Excel workbook with your account order and per-paycheck amounts, a Roth vs. traditional comparison, an on-track check, a start-now calculator, sourced limits, and a check-in for raises, new jobs and open enrollment. Education, not advice: it lays out the trade-offs and you decide.
