# Money Ops Skills

Claude skills for everyday household money decisions, built for households earning under $200K a year.

Each skill turns a common money question into a plan built from your own numbers, usually delivered as an Excel workbook you can keep updating. Every skill looks up the current year's limits and rules instead of relying on memory, and cites where they came from.

## Skills

| Skill | What it helps with | Output |
|---|---|---|
| [retirement-debt-balance](skills/retirement-debt-balance/SKILL.md) | Where your next dollar should go: retirement, debt, emergency fund, kids' college or other goals | Excel workbook: priority ladder, debt payoff schedule, assumptions, monthly check-in |

More skills coming.

## How to use

1. Download the skill folder you want from `skills/`.
2. Upload it to Claude as a skill, or add it to your Claude Code skills folder.
3. Ask Claude your money question in plain words, for example: "I have $400 extra a month. Should it go to my credit card or my 401(k)?"

## Good to know

- These skills offer financial education, not financial advice. They lay out the trade-offs and the math so you can decide.
- They never recommend specific funds, stocks or providers.
- For a business, a large windfall, complex taxes or being close to retirement, talk to a fee-only fiduciary planner.
